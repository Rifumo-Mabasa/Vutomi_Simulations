Vutomi Simulation is a facilitated, experiential learning platform designed to help individuals and teams navigate real-life and workplace decisions.

Developed by MabPark Holdings, Vutomi uses a customisable simulation board game to create insight, reflection, and meaningful conversation without lectures, pressure, or performance anxiety.

It’s not just a game. It’s a structured experience.

How Sessions Work

THE ESSENCE OF VUTOMI

Vutomi means “Life” in Xitsonga.

That’s exactly what the experience reflects.

We believe meaningful growth happens when people are given space to reflect honestly, engage openly, and learn through experience.

Our vision is to become Africa’s leading life-simulation platform, using game-based learning and storytelling to spark growth, reflection, and transformation—both personally and professionally.

OUR METHODOLOGY

What We Do

At the core of Vutomi is a real-life decision-making simulation. Participants engage in scenarios that mirror everyday workplace and life challenges—including communication, money, responsibility, leadership, stress, and values.

Through guided gameplay and facilitated reflection, teams begin to:

  • Understand how decisions impact outcomes
  • Recognise behavioural patterns and blind spots
  • Appreciate different perspectives
  • Improve communication and alignment

This process creates insight that feels natural, not forced.
